## AUDIT

## Usability

19 issues · 0 critical · 5 major · 13 minor · 1 suggestion

The portfolio demonstrates a high level of visual polish and professional branding, with effective use of imagery and clear sectioning. However, the user experience is hindered by significant layout inconsistencies, particularly in grid alignments and vertical spacing, which lead to an excessively long page and increased cognitive load. Improving the density of information and standardizing interactive elements like buttons and links would greatly enhance the site's usability.
