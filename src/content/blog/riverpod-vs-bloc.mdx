## The State Management Dilemma

Choosing the right state management solution is one of the most important architectural decisions in Flutter development.

## Riverpod

Riverpod is a complete rewrite of Provider that addresses many of its limitations:

- **Compile-time safety** — Catch errors before runtime
- **No BuildContext dependency** — Access providers anywhere
- **Auto-dispose** — Automatic cleanup of unused providers
- **Family modifiers** — Parameterized providers

```dart
final userProvider = FutureProvider<User>((ref) async {
  final repository = ref.watch(userRepositoryProvider);
  return repository.getCurrentUser();
});
```

## Bloc

Bloc (Business Logic Component) follows a predictable event-driven pattern:

- **Clear separation** — Events, States, and Bloc classes
- **Testability** — Easy to unit test business logic
- **Predictability** — Unidirectional data flow
- **DevTools** — Excellent debugging with BlocObserver

## When to Use What

| Criteria | Riverpod | Bloc |
|----------|----------|------|
| Learning curve | Moderate | Steeper |
| Boilerplate | Less | More |
| Testing | Good | Excellent |
| Large teams | Great | Great |
| Small projects | Ideal | Overkill |

## My Recommendation

For most production applications, I recommend **Riverpod** for its simplicity and compile-time safety. Use **Bloc** when you need strict event-driven architecture or your team is already familiar with the pattern.
